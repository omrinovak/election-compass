## A. Background

Keren Turner (previously "קרן טרנר-אייל") is a career civil servant, not a career politician. She began in the Treasury's Budget Department as transportation coordinator, moved to the Transportation Ministry (deputy director-general, then director-general from 2016), and served as Director-General of the Finance Ministry under Minister Israel Katz from June to October 2020 before resigning after under six months.
- https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%9F_%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A8
- https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/808775
- https://www.calcalist.co.il/local/articles/0,7340,L-3825251,00.html

She led the Tel Aviv light rail project during her transportation-ministry tenure (Calcalist), then spent roughly five years in the private sector before joining Naftali Bennett's party for the 2026 election — running, as web-search coverage notes, against people who were formerly her own ministerial bosses.
- https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/hypwb2u311x
- https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1308755

## B. Substantial interview / public statement (2025–2026)

**"120 ואחת" (Ynet podcast), interviewer not independently confirmed by article date.** Turner disclosed she has taken psychiatric medication for 15 years: "אני 15 שנה לוקחת כדורים" (I've been taking pills for 15 years), adding it "didn't change who I am, but made me quieter" — made in direct response to MK Edith Silman mocking PM (at the time) Bennett's own medication use. She also said: "המדינה בפוסט-טראומה וסילמן לועגת?" (the country is in post-trauma and Silman is mocking?), framing the nation as collectively traumatized ("הורים שולחים ילדים למלחמה, הורים קברו את ילדיהם" — parents send children to war, parents buried their children).
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/120andone/article/h1z0whljfe
- Follow-up coverage of party reaction: https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hjm00g1171fe (Naftali Bennett: "בפתיחות האמיצה שלך ובכנות שלך את מעניקה כוח ואומץ למאות אלפי נשים וגברים, נערות ונערים... שמתמודדים עם ה'סטיגמה' הזו בשקט"; MK Sheli Tal Miron said seeking mental-health treatment "is not weakness, but strength.")

In the same interview cycle, Turner criticized political appointments in the civil service: "שיקולים פוליטיים באמת רמסו את המקצועיות" (political considerations really trampled on professionalism), referencing her own Treasury-ministry experience, and said "לא קיבלנו לא ממשל ולא ביטחון" (we got neither governance nor security) in assessing the current government's performance. She also stated that, in the foreseeable future, a Palestinian state will not be established, and — discussing Haredi workforce/military integration — said Netanyahu "is selling our future" ("מוכר לנו את העתיד").
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/120andone/article/h1z0whljfe

A subsequent radio interview (103fm, "שבע תשע") reinforced that "המצב הנוכחי אינו יכול להימשך" (the current situation cannot continue).
- https://103fm.maariv.co.il/programs/media.aspx?ZrqvnVq=KGHDKF&c41t4nzVQ=GKE

## C. External, named quotes about Turner

**Positive:** Naftali Bennett (party leader), reacting to her medication disclosure: "קרן, בפתיחות האמיצה שלך ובכנות שלך את מעניקה כוח ואומץ למאות אלפי נשים וגברים, נערות ונערים, שמתמודדים עם ה'סטיגמה' הזו בשקט. אלה האנשים שאנחנו זקוקים להם בהנהגת המדינה. בקרוב עם ישראל כולו יזכה בך כנבחרת ציבור" (Keren, through your courageous openness and honesty you give strength and courage to hundreds of thousands of women and men, girls and boys, who quietly struggle with this "stigma". These are the people we need in national leadership. Soon all of Israel will have the privilege of you as an elected representative).
