## Bio

Age 43; resident of Shoham; married with four children. Former CEO of the "Acharai!" (אחריי!) movement and lead architect of the IDF/Defense Ministry's Haredi-integration service framework. Built "Kodkod" (קודקוד), a pioneering program integrating Haredi personnel into Unit 8200 (the party bio states roughly a third of Haredi IDF enlistees now come through this track), and later "Ma'alot Tzur" (מעלות צור), a framework for integrating Hasidic men into low-tech IDF roles; founded the "Anashim" (אנש"ים) administration, which became the central government body promoting Haredi integration into military service. Author of multiple versions of the "Service for All" (שירות לכל) bill, a comprehensive proposed framework for universal mandatory service. Writes for the journal "HaShiloach." Joined Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" party and was placed 11th on the final list, where the party has designated her as its lead voice on the service-framework issue.
- https://yasharwitheisenkot.com/team-member/inbar-harush-giti/
- https://hashiloach.org.il/author/ihgiti/
