## Bio

Born September 13, 1958, in Osifiya, to a Druze-Israeli family (his father headed the Osifiya local council, 1965–1988). Resident of Osifiya; married with three children. Retired IDF Major General (Aluf). Enlisted in 1977; over roughly four decades held a series of senior security roles including deputy military governor of Jenin and Tulkarem, military governor of Tulkarem, head of the Coordination and Liaison Administration in Gaza, deputy Coordinator of Government Activities in the Territories (COGAT), head of the Civil Administration in Judea and Samaria (2005–2007), head of the Land Crossing Authority, and — after returning to service and being promoted to Major General in 2018 — **Coordinator of Government Activities in the Territories, 2018–2021**. Combat/operational experience across the First Lebanon War, First and Second Intifadas, Second Lebanon War, and Operation Protective Edge. Later chaired the Druze Heritage Center in Israel. Holds a B.A. in political science and Middle East history and an M.A. in international relations. Joined Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" party, announced 2026-07-22, and was placed 13th on the final list.
