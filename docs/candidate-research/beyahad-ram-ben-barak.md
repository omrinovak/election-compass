## A. Background

Ram Ben-Barak is a sitting Member of Knesset for Yesh Atid (in the Knesset since January 2018, previously representing Blue and White before Yesh Atid's later independent lists), and chaired the Knesset's Foreign Affairs and Security Committee (June 2021–November 2022). Before politics he served roughly 30 years in Israeli intelligence, including as head of the Mossad's "Keshet" (Bow/Arrow) division and as Deputy Director of the Mossad (2009–2011), and later as Director-General of the Ministry of Intelligence and of the Ministry of Strategic Affairs. In 2023 he ran against Yair Lapid for the Yesh Atid party leadership before the two men's parties later merged into "ביחד." He is now placed 12th on the merged list.

## B. Direct quotes / public statements (2025–2026)

**On his list placement, reported by Mako/N12, September 6, 2026.** Asked about being placed 12th rather than higher, Ben-Barak said: "אם אגיד שאני מרוצה אשמע לא אמין" (If I said I were satisfied, I'd sound dishonest), but added: "המטרה חשובה מהאדם" (The goal matters more than the individual) and, on colleagues who left the merged framework over list positioning: "כל אחד שעזב זה כמו דקירה בלב" (Every person who left felt like a stab in the heart). Asked about tension with Lapid over positioning, he said: "אין בזה שום קורטוב של אמת" (There isn't a grain of truth to that). On the political mission of the merged list: "אנחנו נעשה את מה שצריך בשביל להעיף את הממשלה הגרועה הזאת" (We'll do what's necessary to remove this bad government).
- https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-d08c3fb1c5770a1026.htm

**On Iran and the security establishment, reported by Walla News, February 12, 2026.** On what he described as a strategic window regarding Iran's missile program: "יש כאן הזדמנות שלא הייתה לנו עד היום, ולא תחזור בעשור הקרוב" (There's an opportunity here we haven't had until now, and it won't return in the coming decade), and: "אנחנו לא יכולים לחיות עם איום טילים בכל רגע נתון" (We cannot live under a missile threat at every given moment). On Gaza policy: "נכשלנו בעזה, לא מבחינה צבאית אלא בזה שלא יזמנו משהו מדיני" (We failed in Gaza — not militarily, but in that we didn't initiate any diplomatic move). On Netanyahu specifically: "ראש הממשלה איש חכם מאוד, אבל לא עשה שום מהלך אמיתי מדיני" (The Prime Minister is a very smart man, but he made no real diplomatic move).
- https://news.walla.co.il/item/3816251

## Caveats

- Older statements (2022–2024) exist on the public record — including support for repealing the Nation-State Law and openness to a coalition with Ra'am (Kikar HaShabat/Arutz 7, July 2022) and detailed hostage-deal negotiating comments (Walla, July 2024) — but were excluded from the scored table because they fall outside the preferred 2025–2026 window and predate the Bennett-Lapid merger; they are noted here for transparency only, not scored.
- A further 2024-dated Iran-related interview (Kikar HaShabat, April 8, 2024: "אני פחות מודאג מהמצב של עימות עם איראן") shows a less urgent tone on Iran than the February 2026 Walla interview quoted above; this is flagged as a possible shift in emphasis over time rather than resolved into a single score, and the more recent 2026 statement was weighted preferentially.
- Given his long pre-2025 public record as a sitting MK and former senior intelligence official, this file deliberately restricts itself to statements from the 2025–2026 window (with two clearly-dated exceptions noted above) rather than surveying his entire Knesset tenure, consistent with the project's recency-focused methodology.
