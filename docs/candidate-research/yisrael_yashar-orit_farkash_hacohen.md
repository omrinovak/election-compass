## Bio

Jerusalem-born, 55, married with four children; law degree from Hebrew University, clerked at the Supreme Court. **CEO of the Israel Electric Corporation, 2011–2015** — the only woman to date to hold that post — where she advanced private electricity generation and Israel's early solar-energy development. Entered politics with Benny Gantz's Resilience/Blue and White in 2019. Held three ministerial posts across the 2020–2022 governments: **Minister for Strategic Affairs** (May–Nov 2020), **Minister of Tourism** (Oct 2020–June 2021), and **Minister of Innovation, Science and Technology** (June 2021–Dec 2022), where she advanced AI initiatives and tech cooperation with the UAE and Bahrain under the Abraham Accords. Currently deputy Knesset speaker and chair of the Knesset's AI/advanced-technology subcommittee.

In July 2025 she announced her intent to leave Gantz's Mahaneh Mamlachti and align with Gadi Eisenkot, initially remaining in the faction (at Gantz's request) so as not to strengthen the sitting coalition by resignation; she formally departed in 2025 once Matan Kahana and others had already left. She was placed 3rd on the final "ישר" list.
