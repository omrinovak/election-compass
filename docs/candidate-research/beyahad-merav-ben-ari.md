## A. Background

Merav Ben Ari has been a Yesh Atid Knesset member for approximately nine years, chairs (or has chaired) the Knesset's Internal Security Committee, and served as the opposition's parliamentary/coalition coordinator during the current Knesset term. Party materials note she was recognized multiple times as the most "socially" active coalition-era MK for programs aimed at vulnerable populations.

## B. Substantial interviews / statements (2025–2026)

**Ynet, July 17, 2026** (on her role as opposition coalition coordinator). Ben Ari, on the difficulty of managing an ideologically diverse opposition bloc post-October 7: "היינו ימנים, שמאלנים, ערבים, יהודים. לא הצלחתי להכניס אותם לחדר" (We were rightists, leftists, Arabs, Jews. I couldn't get them into the same room), contrasting this with what she called a coalition that stayed "glued to their seats." She also argued the opposition passed more legislation than the governing coalition despite its numeric minority (52 vs. 68 seats) and criticized skewed media coverage of coalition vs. opposition (68%/32%).
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/yokra14833983

**X (Twitter) post reported by Israel Hayom, April 10, 2026**, responding to an Aryeh Deri interview: "משקר בלי למצמץ, ובעיקר עסקן קטן חסר לב" (lying without blinking, and above all a small-hearted petty operator), adding: he leads a party that "once had compassion and sensitivity" and "brought hope to the weakest," now led by a man who "stole millions for his tax-exempt institutions before the holiday" and "leads blanket evasion."
- https://www.israelhayom.co.il/news/politics/article/20296273

**On the Haredi conscription-exemption law**, per Knesset/party-linked coverage: Ben Ari has stated that a genuine draft law cannot pass as long as Haredi parties remain in the governing coalition, framing Haredi enforcement as something Haredi parties themselves resist being subject to.
- https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026_q3/Article-612acbf5b3270a1026.htm
