## A. Background

Michal Hirsh Negari is an education and public-administration professional. She headed the Education Department of the Ra'anana municipality (2006–2013) and then served as the municipality's Director-General/city manager (2013–2019). She is a co-founder of "EDU NOW" and currently runs training programs for local-authority CEOs/managers nationwide. She is 59 and lives in Herzliya; she is also a board/steering-committee member of the "Pnima" civil-society movement.

She is the mother of Roy Negari z"l, a staff sergeant in the IDF's Lotar counter-terrorism unit, who was killed on October 7, 2023 at Kibbutz Be'eri after — per the announcement coverage — deploying himself and his team into the kibbutz without waiting for orders, moving house-to-house under fire to evacuate civilians, and falling in battle while saving lives.
