## A. Background

Yonatan Shalu (born October 13, 2002) is a reservist fighter in the Maglan special-forces unit, discharged from regular IDF service in 2024. In 2023 he co-founded (with Ido Keren) the organization "כתף אל כתף" (Shoulder to Shoulder), which organizes reservists to campaign against Haredi draft-exemption legislation and for "equality of the burden" across all sectors of Israeli society. He joined Naftali Bennett's party ("בנט 2026") in April 2026 and was placed 10th on the merged "ביחד" list, making him — at around 23 years old — the youngest candidate on a realistic seat, a fact that generated public controversy (see External views, below).

## B. Direct quotes / public statements (2025–2026)

**Knesset committee appearance (reported by Ynet, article dated on/around April 15, 2026 per available metadata).** During a Knesset committee discussion on the draft-exemption law, addressing MK Yinon Azulai, Shalu said: "איבדתי עשרות לוחמים ואתם מדברים על קירוב לבבות?" (I lost dozens of fighters and you're talking about "bringing hearts closer"?). While being removed from the proceedings he shouted: "אני לחמתי שנה, על מה אתם נלחמים פה?" (I fought for a year — what are you fighting about here?).
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hj11y6s3hwe

**On joining Naftali Bennett's party, reported by Davar (davar1.co.il), April 15, 2026.** Explaining the decision to move from civil activism into politics: "כשראינו שההנהגה לא סופרת אותנו ופועלת כדי לכרות את הענף שאנחנו יושבים עליו" (When we saw that the leadership doesn't count us, and acts to saw off the branch we're sitting on). On the personal cost of the move: "המעבר מפעילות אזרחית לזירה הפוליטית הוא לא פשוט עבורי. יש לו מחירים" (The move from civic activism to the political arena isn't simple for me. It has costs). On reservist life generally: "זה לא רק ימי מילואים, החיים פה בין מילואים לבין שגרה הם כל כך מורכבים" (It's not just reserve-duty days — life here between reserve duty and routine is so complicated).
- https://www.davar1.co.il/671111/

## C. External, named quotes about Shalu

It's unbearable), adding "יונתן ישמש פה למען הדור המיוחד הזה, ולא ירפה עד שיהיה שינוי" (Yonatan will serve here for this special generation, and won't let up until there's change).
