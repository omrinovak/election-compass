# Candidate research: יועז הנדל (Yoaz Hendel)

**Research date:** 2026-09-10
**Status flag:** The party record `beit_tzioni` in `src/data/parties.json` currently lists a single joint leader field `"יועז הנדל וחילי טרופר"`. This is **STALE**. As of 2026-09-06, Hendel and Tropper publicly split. See "Top-level finding" below and in the main reply.

---

## Top-level finding on list status (read first)

The "בית ציוני – המילואימניקים" joint venture between Hendel and Tropper **dissolved on/around September 6, 2026**, just before the candidate-list registration deadline:

- **Chili Tropper** joined Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" (Yisrael Yashar) list, placed in the **6th slot** — a subordinate position, not the PM candidate of that list (Eisenkot is).
  - Sources: [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/skuwxxiuml) (Sept 2026); [Haaretz, 2026-09-06](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-09-06/ty-article/000001a0-75c4-dee1-afea-75cebde20000); [Mako/N12](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2026_q3/Article-41bd566bfb570a1027.htm).
- **Yoaz Hendel** did NOT join Eisenkot or stay merged with Tropper. He instead formed a **separate technical bloc** with economist **Yaron Zlicha** (chairman of the "Economic Party"). Zlicha explicitly described the arrangement as "two heads of two independent parties" (**not** a single unified list with one PM candidate) — i.e., a threshold-passing technical alliance, not a merger.
  - Sources: [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/bjmaqgo00gg); [Haaretz, 2026-09-06](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-09-06/ty-article/000001a0-75c4-dee1-afea-75cebde20000); [Walla](https://news.walla.co.il/item/3866017); [Channel 7](https://www.inn.co.il/news/705730).

**Conclusion:** Neither Hendel nor Tropper is confirmed by press coverage as "the" final PM candidate of a single unified "Beit Tzioni" list — that joint list no longer exists. Hendel remains head of his own party (HaMiluimnikim) in a technical bloc with Zlicha; Tropper is now a down-ballot candidate under Eisenkot's separate list. The `beit_tzioni` party record should likely be restructured or split rather than kept as one joint-leader entry.

**Track record check:** This is NOT a brand-new figure. Hendel has a substantial, multi-term political record predating this list: MK 2019–2022 (Telem → Derech Eretz → Tikva Chadasha), twice Communications Minister (May–Dec 2020; June 2021–Dec 2022), plus a long pre-political career in the security establishment (Shayetet 13, Shin Bet during the Second Intifada) and as a published author/columnist. (Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%96_%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C).)
