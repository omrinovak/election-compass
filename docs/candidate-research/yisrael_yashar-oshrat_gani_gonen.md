## Bio

Age 55; married with four children. Over three decades in senior local-government management: head of the youth/community department and head of the human-capital division at the Kfar Saba municipality, later CEO of that municipality, where she led wide-scale organizational processes and developed municipal education, welfare, and community services. Elected head of the Southern Sharon Regional Council in 2018 (and re-elected; still serving as of joining "ישר"), where her tenure has focused on education, regional economic development, environmental quality, resident services, and innovation. B.A. in informal education, M.A. with honors in educational counseling from Tel Aviv University. Joined Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" party, announced 2026-07-29, retaining her council seat while running, and was placed 9th on the final list.
