## Bio

Born June 17, 1976, Jerusalem; married with four children. Son of former minister Dan Meridor and economist Dr. Liora Meridor. Career economic-policy technocrat: economist and later deputy head of the Finance Ministry's Budget Division; member of the Tzemach Committee on natural-gas policy; Director-General of the Ministry of Energy; **Director of the Finance Ministry's Budget Division, 2017–2020**. He resigned that post and publicly criticized government fiscal conduct at the time. He is currently CFO at Lightricks. Joined Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" party in 2026 and was placed 7th.
- https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%95%D7%9C_%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%93%D7%95%D7%A8
