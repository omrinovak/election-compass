## A. Background

Bruria Neim Arman has served as CEO ("מנכ"לית") of the "ביחד"/Bennett 2026 party since its founding, and is described as the architect of its national field/grassroots operation — reportedly building a network of over 200 local branches ("מטות") and tens of thousands of activists. She is the founder and owner of the community-relations firm "CR קשרי קהילה." Earlier in her career she served as deputy director of community relations at נת"ע (the Tel Aviv-area light-rail operating company), and separately as chief of staff to a Transportation Minister and to a Deputy Defense Minister. She holds a bachelor's and a master's degree in politics/political science. She was announced as one of four new women added to the "ביחד" list, bringing the list's total number of women to 17.
