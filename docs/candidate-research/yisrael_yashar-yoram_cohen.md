## Bio

Born October 15, 1960, Tel Aviv. Career Shin Bet officer: joined 1982 after IDF service in Golani, rose through field coordination, operational desk command, and counter-terrorism roles; deputy Shin Bet director 2005–2010; **Director of the Shin Bet, 2011–2016** (12th director, appointed under Netanyahu). His tenure included the 2012 targeted killing of Hamas commander Ahmad Jabari and the 2014–2015 wave of stabbing/shooting attacks; he backed the Shalit prisoner exchange, saying Israel could manage the resulting security risk.

After retiring he became a security consultant and a vocal public critic of the 2023 judicial overhaul (warning of "disaster") and of Netanyahu's conduct regarding October 7 accountability. He joined Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" party in May 2026 and was placed second on the list, positioned by the party as bringing "35+ years of rich security experience and national-security expertise" (Eisenkot's framing at the joint announcement).
