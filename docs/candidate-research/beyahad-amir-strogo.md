## A. Background

Amir Strogo (age 41, a resident of Kibbutz Karmim, father of four) is the former CEO of "אחריי!" (Achrai), a long-established nonprofit working on social involvement and values-based education among at-risk and periphery youth. On October 7, 2023, he joined the fighting in the western Negev without having received a call-up order, and has since accumulated over 400 days of reserve service as deputy battalion commander in the Maglan special-forces unit during the Gaza war. He joined Naftali Bennett's party in mid-2026 and subsequently founded "סיירת בנט" (Sayeret Bennett), a dedicated field/ground-operations team working specifically in cities and neighborhoods identified as Likud strongholds, aiming to identify local leaders sympathetic to political change.

## B. Direct quotes / public statements (2025–2026)

**Radio interview with Sharon Gal, Galei Yisrael, August 2, 2026, on a potential coalition with Yair Golan's Democrats party.** Pressed by host Sharon Gal on why he would not sharply denounce statements by Democrats-party figures, and asked to draw a clear values "red line" toward potential coalition partners, Strogo said: "אני לא עסוק כרגע בלגנות אנשים. אני ממוקד בדבר אחד בלבד; איך מצעידים את מדינת ישראל קדימה למקום של שפיות ובנייה משותפת" (I'm not currently focused on badmouthing people. I'm focused on one thing only: how to move the State of Israel forward to a place of sanity and joint building). On disagreements with Golan specifically: "אני לא מסכים עם חלק גדול ממה שהוא אומר" (I disagree with a large part of what he says). On the scope of any future partnership: "הוא אכן יהיה שותף שלנו, אבל הוא ממש לא זה שיקבע את הטון בממשלה. השאיפה שלנו היא לראות סביב שולחן הממשלה מפלגות ציוניות שתורמות לביטחון ולכלכלה" (He will indeed be our partner, but he is absolutely not the one who will set the government's tone. Our aspiration is to see Zionist parties around the government table that contribute to security and the economy).
