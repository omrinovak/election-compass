## Bio

Religious-Zionist politician, IDF Lieutenant Colonel (res.), law graduate of Bar-Ilan University's honors program, married to a clinical psychologist, father of four, resident of the religious kibbutz Beit Gamliel. Former MK (Yamina) and **Minister of Religious Services, 2021–2022**, where he led two signature reforms: opening the kashrut-supervision market to competing certified organizations (with then-Finance Minister Avigdor Liberman, 2021) and capping city-rabbi tenure at ten years (2022). A close, 35-year personal friend of Naftali Bennett. He left the "Mamlachti" political orbit associated with Bennett and announced on 2026-04-25 that he would run instead on Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" list, ultimately placed 5th.
