# Candidate research: חילי טרופר (Chili Tropper)

**Research date:** 2026-09-10
**Status flag:** The party record `beit_tzioni` in `src/data/parties.json` currently lists a single joint leader field `"יועז הנדל וחילי טרופר"`. This is **STALE**. As of 2026-09-06, Hendel and Tropper publicly split. See "Top-level finding" below and in the main reply.

---

## Top-level finding on list status (read first)

The "בית ציוני – המילואימניקים" joint venture between Tropper and Hendel **dissolved on/around September 6, 2026**, just before the candidate-list registration deadline:

- **Chili Tropper** joined Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר" (Yisrael Yashar) list, placed in the **6th slot**. Per reporting, this is not a "technical bloc" arrangement — Tropper cannot later split his faction off — and he is promised first choice of ministerial portfolios if Eisenkot forms a government, but **Eisenkot, not Tropper, is the list's PM candidate**.
  - Quote from Tropper on joining: "עכשיו הזמן לחבר את ישראל מבפנים. אחריך גדי!" ("Now is the time to reconnect Israel from within. After you, Gadi!")
  - Eisenkot on Tropper: "an ethical and statesman-like leader," "a partner in faith in responsibility and unity."
  - Sources: [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/skuwxxiuml); [Haaretz, 2026-09-06](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-09-06/ty-article/000001a0-75c4-dee1-afea-75cebde20000).
- **Yoaz Hendel** did not join this arrangement; he separately teamed up with economist Yaron Zlicha as co-heads of a technical bloc.

**Conclusion:** Tropper is confirmed by press coverage to now be a **subordinate down-ballot candidate (6th slot) under Gadi Eisenkot**, not a PM candidate himself and no longer jointly leading a "Beit Tzioni" list with Hendel. The `beit_tzioni` party record in the app is stale on this point and should be updated/split to reflect that Tropper's political home is now Eisenkot's "ישר" list.

**Track record check:** This is NOT a brand-new figure. Tropper has a substantial multi-term record: MK since 2019 (Blue and White, then State Camp/Mamlachti), **Minister of Culture and Sport** (May 2020–Dec 2022) under three different prime ministers (Netanyahu, Bennett, Lapid), briefly **Minister of Science and Technology** (May–June 2021), and **Minister without Portfolio** during the Iron Swords War (Oct 2023–June 2024) before resigning in June 2024. He left Blue and White for Beit Tzioni/Yesodot Yisrael in May 2026. (Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%99_%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%A8).)

## B. Position papers / authored op-eds

**Verified authored op-ed:** "שיעור במנהיגות: דווקא כשחלק גדול מההנהגה אינו מתעלה לגודל השעה" ("A Lesson in Leadership: Especially When Much of the Leadership Does Not Rise to the Occasion"), Maariv Opinions, **2025-11-27**.
URL: https://www.maariv.co.il/news/opinions/article-1256197
Content: Tropper argues that ordinary citizens are showing better leadership than institutional leaders during the crisis, centering the piece on volunteer Anabel Nechemia (whose wounded son has been hospitalized 22 months) and bereaved families Sharit Zusman, Dana Sitton, and Mazi Ilyon. His own words: "דווקא כשחלק גדול מההנהגה אינו מתעלה לגודל השעה, אנשים מן היישוב מתגלים" (precisely when much of leadership fails to rise to the occasion, people from the community reveal themselves); he defines wartime leadership as "מאבק מתמשך על התקווה" (an ongoing struggle for hope).

This satisfies deliverable (B) — a genuine signed long-form piece authored by Tropper himself, not a campaign slogan.
