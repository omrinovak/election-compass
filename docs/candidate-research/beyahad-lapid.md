# Candidate research: יאיר לפיד (Yair Lapid) — רשימת "ביחד"

Researched 2026-09-10. All facts below were verified via live web search/fetch against the cited URLs. Nothing below is inferred, estimated, or invented. Where a source could not be independently confirmed, it is omitted rather than guessed.

## 0. Resolution of the "who is the joint list's PM candidate" question

See the companion file `beyahad-bennett.md` for the full sourcing. Summary: multiple independent, reputable outlets (Hebrew Wikipedia, Ynet, Mako/N12, Kipa, Kore, Srugim, Maariv, Calcalist, Haaretz English) confirm that as of the April 26, 2026 merger announcement and the September 2026 finalized list, **Naftali Bennett is #1 on the list and its Prime Minister candidate, and Yair Lapid is #2**. This is not an equal or ambiguous joint arrangement — Lapid explicitly took the second slot in the merger agreement (some coverage notes Lapid was even willing to accept third place if Gadi Eisenkot joined, which did not happen). The `beyahad.leader` field listing both names jointly and without rank should be treated by the app maintainer as stale on this point.

## A. Substantial interviews (2025–2026)

1. **Walla News, "סרי ושלזינגר" (Barak Seri & Yehuda Shlesinger), November 20, 2025.** Lapid: "אין לי אחריות על 7 באוקטובר, ולא על מה שהוביל אליו" (I have no responsibility for October 7, nor for what led to it) — argues his own prior government prepared differently and that Netanyahu's government ignored warnings while focused on judicial overhaul. Said Netanyahu could not serve as PM again but could conceivably hold a security portfolio under someone else. Called the proposed Haredi conscription-exemption law a "betrayal of Zionism" and threatened not to form a coalition with Haredi parties absent his own recruitment framework. Criticized Smotrich's proposed media legislation as democratic erosion turning legitimate media into "state propaganda." Signaled flexibility with Bennett on forming a government together to remove Netanyahu.
   - https://news.walla.co.il/item/3795799

2. **ynet political podcast "120 ואחת," hosted by Moran Azoulay** (article summarizing/quoting the episode). Lapid, on the pre-October 7 intelligence briefing with Netanyahu: "הוא היה משועמם" (he was bored) / "אני נבהלתי. הוא היה משועמם" (I was alarmed. He was bored); "נתניהו קיבל כל אזהרה אפשרית... אף ראש ממשלה אחר לא היה מתעלם מהחומרים האלה" (Netanyahu received every possible warning... no other PM would have ignored this material); on his own contemporaneous press conference: "מעולם לא קיימתי מסיבת עיתונאים כזו קודם או אחרי בחיי" (I never held a press conference like that before or since in my life).
   - https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/sy00dq2r00me
   - Note: the exact air date of this podcast episode could not be independently confirmed and is therefore not stated; the article discussing it is contemporaneous political reporting, treated here as a substantial recorded interview rather than a soundbite.

## B. Position papers / policy plans authored/presented by Lapid personally

- **26-step economic rescue plan, presented by Lapid as Opposition Leader/Yesh Atid chair, March 11, 2025** (Walla Finance). Lapid: "המשבר הכלכלי הנוכחי נובע מניהול כושל ומשחיתות פוליטית בהיקפים חסרי תקדים" (the current economic crisis stems from failed management and unprecedented-scale political corruption). Two headline planks: eliminating coalition funds and redundant ministries; cutting taxes for the middle class. Also proposed a dedicated "cost of living ministry," a "war on bureaucracy" commissioner, and expanded vocational training for Haredi employment.
  - https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3733485
  - Contemporaneous critical analysis calling the plan light on specifics: Calcalist, "קלישאות וסיסמאות חלולות: התוכנית הכלכלית של לפיד לא מציבה אלטרנטיבה" — https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/syfp4kajye

- **UN General Assembly speech, September 2022** (older, outside the preferred 2025–2026 window, included because it is Lapid's own clearly-stated, still-unretracted position on the two-state issue and no more recent restatement/repudiation was found in this research): "הסכם של שתי מדינות לשני עמים הוא הדבר הנכון לביטחון ישראל, לכלכלה הישראלית ולעתיד ילדינו" (a two-state-for-two-peoples agreement is the right thing for Israel's security, its economy, and the future of our children), conditioned on the future Palestinian state being non-militarized and non-terror-supporting.
  - https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/sysggz5bj
