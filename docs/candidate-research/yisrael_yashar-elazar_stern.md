## Bio

Born August 25, 1956. Retired IDF Major General (Aluf). Long career centered on personnel policy, religion-state relations, and conscription equality: as head of the IDF Manpower Directorate, worked to lower enlistment barriers (including for those with criminal records) to broaden who could serve, and pushed to reduce the influence of homogeneous religious-only units and yeshiva exemptions within the army, generating controversy within religious-Zionist circles at the time. Later served as a government minister and as a Knesset member (previously with Hatnua/Tkuma-era lists and, most recently, Yesh Atid). Co-founded the "Nation, Religion and State" caucus with Isaac Herzog in 2013 to advance the Gavison-Medan covenant on religious-secular relations, and initiated the 2014 government conversion-reform decision establishing three-judge municipal-rabbi-led conversion courts. Announced on 2026-08-24 that he was leaving Yesh Atid, after 11 years with the party, to join Gadi Eisenkot's "ישר," resigning his Knesset seat on 2026-09-07; former MK Roi Falkman joined Eisenkot shortly after him. Placed 12th on the final "ישר" list.
