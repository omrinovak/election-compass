## A. Background

Etan Ginzburg is a lawyer who served as Mayor of Ra'anana (elected 2018) before being elected to the Knesset in 2019 as a member of Benny Gantz's "Blue and White" (Kachol Lavan) party. In the Knesset he served as Communications Minister, Deputy Speaker of the Knesset, and chaired a Knesset committee.
- https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%9F_%D7%92%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%96%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%92
- https://kachollavan.org.il/member/6518-2/

On May 5, 2026, Ginzburg informed Benny Gantz he was leaving Blue and White after Gantz had summoned him upon learning he was in talks with other parties and given him an ultimatum to declare his intentions.
- https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-05-05/ty-article/0000019d-f784-ddfa-a5bf-f79da7dd0000
- https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-96683c7b017fd91026.htm

Around his departure, Ginzburg was quoted (per search-synthesized coverage of the resignation) stating: "לצערי, יכולתה של המסגרת הפוליטית בצורתה הנוכחית להמשיך ולהביא לשינוי הנדרש במדינה פחתה. והשינוי בעת הזאת הכרחי ונדרש על ידי המציאות" (Unfortunately, the political framework's ability, in its current form, to keep bringing about the change the country needs has diminished. And the change needed now is essential and demanded by reality).
- https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%9F_%D7%92%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%96%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%92

He announced joining Naftali Bennett's "ביחד" list on August 5, 2026.
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hjla8ox8zx
- https://news.walla.co.il/item/3858693

## B. Substantial statement (2025–2026)

**On joining Beyahad, August 5, 2026** (Ynet). Ginzburg: "לאחר ארבע שנים קשות, לממשלה הבאה לא יהיו רגעי חסד" (After four difficult years, the next government will have no grace period), stating he was "happy and excited" to join Bennett and the "Beyahad" slate, and emphasizing that the incoming government must act "from day one" to repair the damage of recent years via detailed, pre-prepared work plans rather than "trial and error" — citing his personal familiarity with Bennett's governing record (Ginzburg served in the Knesset partly during Bennett's 2021–22 premiership) as a basis for confidence in that approach.
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hjla8ox8zx
