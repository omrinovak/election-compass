## A. Background

- First elected to the Knesset in 2019 on the joint "Kachol Lavan" (Blue and White) list (position 17 of 35 mandates). Moved to Yesh Atid in January 2021 (positioned 5th on its list). Served as **Minister for Social Equality** in the Bennett–Lapid government (36th government, sworn in June 13, 2021) until the government fell on December 29, 2022.
  - https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%91_%D7%9B%D7%94%D7%9F
- Currently chairs the Knesset "Committee for the Advancement of the Status of Women and Gender Equality" (assumed the chair in November 2025).
  - https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%91_%D7%9B%D7%94%D7%9F
- On February 5, 2026, opposition leader Yair Lapid announced that Yesh Atid will demand the Education portfolio in the next government, with Cohen as the designated candidate for Minister of Education, alongside a detailed national education plan ("עתיד לחינוך" / "Future for Education").
  - https://www.mako.co.il/news-education/2026_q1/Article-78716844b9c2c91027.htm

## B. Position papers / policy plans personally presented by Cohen

- **N12/mako, by Dafna Liel, published 05.02.26.** Cohen and Lapid jointly unveiled the "עתיד לחינוך" plan at a press conference. Cohen's own quote: **"אני רואה בזה משימת חיים, מערכת החינוך היא הבסיס לתיקון הנדרש במדינת ישראל. יש לנו הזדמנות של פעם בדור להסיט את המדינה ממסלול של התרסקות - למסלול של שגשוג"** ("I see this as my life's mission — the education system is the foundation of the correction Israel needs. We have a once-in-a-generation opportunity to divert the country from a path of collapse to a path of prosperity.") Plan specifics attributed to the joint Cohen–Lapid rollout: multi-billion-shekel addition to state (mamlachti) education budgets and teacher salaries; multi-year plan to cut average class size to 20 students; free/subsidized public early-childhood education from birth; a **complete cutoff of state funding to educational institutions that do not teach the full core curriculum (100% ליבה)** — aimed squarely at non-core-curriculum Haredi yeshivas; a politically-insulated "National Education Council"; and a phone ban in grades 1–11.
  - https://www.mako.co.il/news-education/2026_q1/Article-78716844b9c2c91027.htm

## C. Substantial interviews (2025–2026)

1. **Arutz 7 (Channel 7 / INN), published 20.10.25, 21:09.** On the government's Gaza withdrawal and the ceasefire framework: **"היא יצאה מהשטח ואין ואקום. אנחנו רואים את חמאס מבסס ברגעים אלה את שלטונו"** ("We left the territory and there is no vacuum — we're watching Hamas consolidate its rule right now"). On Hamas and humanitarian aid optics: **"משתלט על הסיוע ההומניטארי ועל הנרטיב של ההסברה כלפי כל העולם"** (Hamas "is seizing control of the humanitarian aid and the narrative directed at the whole world"). On the deal framework: **"נגררה להסכם הכולל מדינות כמו טורקיה וקטאר החפצות בשיקומו של חמאס"** (Israel "was dragged into a deal that includes states like Turkey and Qatar that want Hamas's rehabilitation"). Cohen's overall argument: the government failed to hold the political discussion needed to build a moderate, non-Hamas alternative before withdrawing.
   - https://www.inn.co.il/news/681220

2. **Ynet, "120 ואחת" article/podcast summary (exact air date not independently confirmed; treated here, per the same convention used in the companion Lapid research file, as a substantial recorded statement rather than a soundbite).** Discussing an organized online harassment campaign ("מכונת הרעל") targeting her and others, and a car-into-protesters incident in Ra'anana she characterized as an organized, socially-incited lynch: **"אני חיה בתחושה שזה ייגמר ברצח. ההסתה הזו וכל תאוריות הקונספירציה על בגידה מבפנים יגרמו בסוף לרצח"** ("I live with the feeling this will end in murder. This incitement and all these conspiracy theories about internal betrayal will ultimately lead to murder.") She drew an explicit parallel to the 1983 assassination of Emil Grunzweig.
   - https://www.ynet.co.il/news/120andone/article/hktkz6kwee
