## Bio

Age 51; married to Moshe, mother of seven; resident of Jerusalem. Architect and urban planner; senior public-service manager; former deputy director-general at the Ministry of Heritage. Since October 7, has led the government's strategic program for documentation, heritage, and commemoration in the "Tekuma" region (the Gaza-envelope communities), including building partnerships in Israel and abroad and working with philanthropic funds. She is the mother of First Sergeant Aner Shapira z"l, a Nahal reconnaissance fighter killed on October 7, 2023, after repeatedly catching and throwing back grenades thrown by Hamas terrorists into the shelter at the Re'im party site, saving the lives of others sheltering with him; she founded and runs an association in his memory. She resigned from her Ministry of Heritage post to enter politics, initially as a candidate on the "Bayit Tzioni-Miluimnikim" list led by Chili Tropper and Yoaz Hendel, and moved with Tropper into Eisenkot's "ישר" when that list merged into it, ultimately placed 10th.
