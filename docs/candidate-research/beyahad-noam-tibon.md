## A. Background

Noam Tibon is a retired IDF Major General ("אלוף במיל'"), former commander of the 91st ("Galilee") Division and reportedly of Home Front/Northern Command-area posts. He became nationally prominent for his actions on October 7, 2023: after his son Amir (a journalist living in Kibbutz Nahal Oz/Nir Oz) called to report terrorists outside the house, Tibon and his wife Gali drove from Tel Aviv into the combat zone, linked up with an IDF Maglan special-forces unit and reservist Israel Ziv, and helped rescue his son's family and other residents; he also helped rescue Nova festival survivors and assisted wounded soldiers.
- https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%A0%D7%A2%D7%9D_%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%9F

After October 7 he led a "brigadier-generals' project" pairing retired generals with southern communities, was a prominent organizer of "equality of the burden" (military-service-sharing) protests, and was a public advocate for a state commission of inquiry into the failures of October 7. He announced joining Yesh Atid on November 18, 2025.
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hj6ciq5ewl
- https://www.srugim.co.il/news/1297333

## B. Substantial interviews / public statements (2025–2026)

**Announcement of joining Yesh Atid, November 18, 2025** (Ynet, reported same-day; corroborated by Israel Hayom). Tibon: "המצב הביטחוני הגרוע הוא הסיבה" שאני מצטרף ליש עתיד, "לתקן ולהחזיר ביטחון לאזרחי ישראל" (The poor security situation is the reason I'm joining Yesh Atid — to fix things and restore security to Israel's citizens). He pledged: "הממשלה הבאה תקים ועדת חקירה ממלכתית ביום הראשון" (the next government will establish a state commission of inquiry on day one), and demanded accountability — "מי שאחראי לכשל צריך ללכת הביתה" (whoever is responsible for the failure must go home) — naming "כל ראשות הצבא, כל ראשי המודיעין וממשלת הכישלון של נתניהו" (the entire military top brass, all intelligence chiefs, and Netanyahu's government of failure). He also criticized the Haredi conscription-exemption bill as weakening the IDF at a time it needs roughly 12,000 additional soldiers.
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hj6ciq5ewl
- https://www.israelhayom.co.il/news/politics/article/19271922 (independent corroboration of the joining announcement and rationale — could not be fetched directly due to access restriction, but corroborated via search-result summary)

**Radio interview (103fm), reported by Channel 7/Arutz Sheva, undated precisely within available metadata but discussing an active hostage-deal dispute.** Tibon accused PM Netanyahu directly: "זה לא רק חמאס סרבן, אלא גם ראש הממשלה" (It's not just a recalcitrant Hamas, but also the Prime Minister), and: "רה"מ לא מתפקד, עסוק אך ורק בהישרדות הפוליטית שלו וגם משקר לצערי הרב" (The PM isn't functioning, is occupied solely with his own political survival, and is also, regrettably, lying). On negotiating strategy: "אם החטופים חשובים לנו, אנחנו צריכים להתגמש בטווח הקצר ולקבל דברים שלא נוח לנו" (If the hostages matter to us, we need to be flexible in the short term and accept things that are uncomfortable for us).
- https://www.inn.co.il/news/647521
- Note: the precise 2025-vs-2024 date of this specific interview could not be independently confirmed with full confidence in the time available; it is included because it is consistent with, and amplifies, his subsequent dated November 2025 and April 2025 statements below, but flagged for reduced confidence on recency.

**Public call for mass demonstration, reported around April 22, 2025** (Ynet). Tibon: "צאו והצטרפו אלינו להפגנת ענק" (Come out and join us for a giant demonstration), arguing the war was "driven by political considerations rather than security ones" and had to end because forces were exhausted, with reservists serving hundreds of days at the cost of jobs and families in what he called "an endless war without purpose in Gaza," and that ending it and returning all hostages reflected the will of roughly 80% of Israeli citizens.
- https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hkzwip7iex
