## A. Background

Liran Avisher Ben-Horin is an attorney who served as the first female Director-General of Israel's Communications Ministry, from June 2020 (appointed by Minister Yoaz Hendel) to March 2023. In that role she led legislation enabling fiber-optic ("optic fiber") rollout for faster internet and ended the regulatory separation between telecom infrastructure providers and service providers, among other reforms.
- https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%9F_%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8_%D7%91%D7%9F-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9F
- https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001332217

After leaving government, she led a "Tikun Israel" (Fix Israel) policy project for former PM Naftali Bennett, convening roughly 250 senior figures to produce 22 work programs; she joined Keystone, an infrastructure investment fund, in December 2023. She joined Bennett's "ביחד" list alongside Keren Turner.
